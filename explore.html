How this works

  • The table is read where it lives: the module asks Hugging Face for byte ranges of the Lance files, and the vectors of up to 5,000 rows (ten windows of 500 rows, spread over the table) are laid out with PCA in a Web Worker.
  • Colors are the dataset's own labels when it has a label column; otherwise regions of the layout, named by their captions.
Make a dataset like this with pylance 
import lance, pyarrow as pa
emb = pa.FixedSizeListArray.from_arrays(pa.array(vectors.ravel(), pa.float32()), 512)
lance.write_dataset(pa.table({"image": pa.array(jpegs, pa.binary()), "label_name": labels, "image_emb": emb}), "my_images.lance")
